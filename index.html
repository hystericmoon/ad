Questions

About watches, buying and colours.

See the Google Play page

Bought on Google Play from your Android phone. Runs on Wear OS 5 and later.

Which watches can run these faces?

Watches on Wear OS 5 or later, which includes recent Galaxy Watch and Pixel Watch models.

How do I put a face on my watch?

Buy it on Google Play from your phone and choose your watch as the device. It then shows in the watch's face list.

Is the price one payment or monthly?

One payment. Each face is a one-time purchase on Google Play.

Can I change a face's colours?

Yes. Most faces come in five colourways or more, picked in the watch's face settings.

Are prices the same everywhere?

The prices here are Google Play's US prices. Your store shows the price in your currency.
