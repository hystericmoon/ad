## Owner decisions (keep)

- Hosni Rachmani is the owner; faces listed under "Hosni Rachmani" and "Active Design Studio" are all his.
- Space / futuristic, technical instrument tone, near-black monochrome; neon only inside the face renders.
- Logo and buttons white, never coloured. Fonts: Sora (display) + Manrope (text); Geist Mono for captions and dates.
- One page. Structure after linear.app/homepage, surfaces after docs.cosmos.network. Copy neither site's words, images or brand; the halftone is drawn by `js/site.js`.
- All 63 faces sit in one grid with style tabs (All, Digital, Hybrid, Analog), search by name and a Free only filter.
- No New faces section and no Prices section (owner removed both). No fixed prices anywhere (Google Play prices by region). No testimonials or customer logos.
- Halftone is grey, not tinted.
- Faces shown on Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra and Galaxy Watch9 press renders (check licence before going live).
- Static HTML/CSS/JS; deploy target Vercel or Netlify.
- Dropped: Kobra component look, React Bits Pro, the separate catalog page.

## Files

- `index.html` the page; counts, tabs, the grid and the hero watches are generated by `node tools/build.mjs` from `data/faces.json`.
- `css/site.css`, `css/watch.css`, `js/site.js`.
